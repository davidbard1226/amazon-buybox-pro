📤 Push prices to Amazon (no API needed)

1. Click Generate flat file in the Repricer tab (or below) — it downloads a TSV with your SKUs and new prices.
2. Go to Seller Central → Inventory → Add Products via Upload.
3. Choose the Price & Inventory template, upload the file, review the report.
4. Amazon applies the prices — usually within a few minutes.
⚠ If Amazon rejects the file, open the official Price & Inventory template (download it from the upload page), paste your sku and price columns into it, and re-upload. Some marketplaces require the exact template headers.

🤖 Amazon Automate Pricing (built-in repricer)

Amazon's own repricer can do the chasing for you — no uploads needed:
1. Seller Central → Pricing → Automate Pricing.
2. Create a rule: Match Buy Box Price or Beat Buy Box Price by R X, with a minimum price (your floor) and optional maximum price.
3. Assign SKUs to the rule.
This dashboard then becomes your monitoring + alert layer: it watches win/lose, competitor prices, and pings Telegram when you lose the box or a competitor undercuts.
💡 Recommended combo: Automate Pricing handles the fast chasing; this dashboard handles the strategy, floors, locks, and alerts.
